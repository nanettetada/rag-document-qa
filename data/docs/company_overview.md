# Aurora Robotics — Company Overview

Aurora Robotics is a fictional consumer-robotics company founded in 2019 and headquartered in Cape Town, South Africa, with offices in Lisbon and Singapore. The company designs autonomous aerial and ground robots for hobbyists, prosumers, and small commercial operators.

## Mission

To make autonomous robotics affordable, safe, and useful for everyday creators — photographers, farmers, surveyors, and educators.

## Product lines

1. **Aurora-X1** — flagship quad-rotor camera drone, 4K stabilised gimbal, 32-minute flight time
2. **Aurora-X1 Pro** — professional variant with thermal camera and RTK GPS module
3. **Aurora Rover Mini** — small autonomous ground rover used by schools and educators
4. **Aurora Sky Dock** — automated charging dock for the X1 series

## Headcount and locations

As of 2025, Aurora Robotics employs 184 people across three offices. The Cape Town HQ houses engineering and operations, Lisbon focuses on European sales and support, and Singapore covers Asia-Pacific distribution.

## Business model

Aurora Robotics sells direct-to-consumer through its website and via a network of accredited resellers. A subscription service, **Aurora Care+**, provides accidental-damage cover and a 48-hour replacement guarantee for an additional monthly fee.

## Sustainability commitments

All Aurora packaging is fibre-based and FSC-certified. The Cape Town HQ runs on 92% renewable electricity and the company offsets the remaining carbon footprint through verified reforestation projects in the Western Cape.

# Aurora-X1 Product Handbook

## What's in the box

- 1 × Aurora-X1 drone
- 1 × Aurora controller (Bluetooth 5.2)
- 2 × intelligent flight batteries
- 1 × charging hub
- 4 × spare propellers
- 1 × quick-start card
- 1 × USB-C cable

## Battery

The Aurora-X1 ships with the **AB-32** intelligent flight battery: 3,850 mAh, 11.55 V, 44.5 Wh. A fully charged battery delivers up to **32 minutes** of hover flight in calm conditions or roughly **27 minutes** of mixed-use cinematic flight.

### Charging times

| Charger | Single battery | Both batteries (sequential) |
|---|---|---|
| Included 65 W USB-C charger | 55 minutes | 110 minutes |
| Aurora Sky Dock | 50 minutes | parallel |

### Storage recommendations

For storage longer than two weeks, discharge each battery to approximately 50% capacity. Keep the batteries between 5°C and 25°C in a dry environment. The battery firmware will automatically discharge a fully charged battery to safe storage voltage after 10 idle days.

## Camera

- 1-inch CMOS sensor, 20 MP
- 4K video at 60 fps, 1080p at 120 fps
- 3-axis mechanical gimbal
- Adjustable aperture f/2.8 – f/11

## Connectivity

- 2.4 GHz / 5.8 GHz auto-switching
- Maximum unobstructed control range: 8 km (FCC), 5 km (CE), 4 km (SRRC)
- Live preview at 1080p / 30 fps from the drone to the controller

## Flight modes

- **Cine** — slowed control inputs for smooth cinematic shots
- **Sport** — top speed 19 m/s for chase shots and racing
- **Tripod** — ultra-fine control for indoor or tight-space flying
- **Follow** — autonomous tracking using onboard vision
- **Waypoint** — predefined GPS flight paths

## Firmware updates

Firmware updates are released roughly every six weeks. Updates are delivered over the Aurora mobile app and take 8–12 minutes per device. Always update both the drone and the controller in the same session.

Aurora Robotics — Limited Warranty Policy

1. WARRANTY PERIOD

The Aurora-X1 drone, the Aurora-X1 Pro, the Aurora Rover Mini, and the Aurora Sky Dock are covered by a 24-month limited manufacturer warranty from the date of purchase. Aurora batteries (AB-32) and accessories such as propellers and cables are covered for 12 months.

2. WHAT IS COVERED

This warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use. Examples include:
  - Motors that fail without external damage
  - Faulty gimbal motors or sensors
  - Manufacturing defects in the airframe
  - Battery cells that fail to hold charge under correct usage

3. WHAT IS NOT COVERED

The warranty does not cover:
  - Accidental damage (crashes, water ingress, drops)
  - Damage caused by unauthorised firmware modifications
  - Cosmetic damage that does not affect performance
  - Damage from operating outside published environmental limits
  - Lost drones (e.g. fly-away events not caused by a documented firmware defect)

4. AURORA CARE+

Customers may purchase Aurora Care+ within 30 days of original purchase. Aurora Care+ extends the warranty to 36 months total, covers accidental damage (with a per-incident excess of USD 49), and provides a 48-hour replacement guarantee in supported regions.

5. HOW TO CLAIM

To file a claim, email support@aurora-robotics.example with the serial number, proof of purchase, and a description of the issue. A support agent will respond within 1 business day. Approved warranty repairs are completed at no cost to the customer; shipping is paid by Aurora Robotics within the country of purchase.

6. RETURNS

In addition to the warranty, Aurora Robotics offers a 14-day no-questions-asked return policy on direct-to-consumer purchases. The product must be returned in its original packaging and in good cosmetic condition.

7. WARRANTY TRANSFERS

The warranty is tied to the original purchaser and cannot be transferred to a subsequent owner unless the device is registered through the official Aurora resale programme.

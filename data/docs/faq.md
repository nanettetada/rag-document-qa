# Aurora Robotics — Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does the Aurora-X1 fly on a single battery?

Up to 32 minutes in calm hover and around 27 minutes of mixed cinematic flight. Cold weather and aggressive flight modes can reduce this by 20–30%.

### Can I fly the Aurora-X1 in the rain?

No. The Aurora-X1 is rated IP44 against light splashes only and is not waterproof. Flying in rain or fog voids the warranty.

### What is the maximum altitude?

The Aurora-X1 has a maximum service ceiling of 6,000 m above sea level. Many countries restrict consumer drones to 120 m above ground level — always check local regulations.

### Can I use third-party batteries?

No. Only the AB-32 intelligent flight battery is supported. Third-party batteries lack the safety telemetry the drone needs to operate and may damage the aircraft.

### Does the Aurora-X1 support DJI accessories?

No. The Aurora ecosystem is independent of DJI's and accessories are not cross-compatible.

### Where can I buy replacement propellers?

Through the Aurora online store or any accredited reseller. A pack of four spare propellers is included in the box; subsequent packs cost USD 12.

### How do I update the firmware?

Open the Aurora mobile app while connected to the drone and the controller. Tap Settings → About → Firmware Update. The update takes 8–12 minutes per device. Do not power off either device during the update.

### Is the camera removable?

The gimbal-and-camera unit is field-swappable and can be replaced as a single module. The standard 1-inch sensor module on the Aurora-X1 cannot be upgraded to the Pro thermal module.

### Can I get a refund?

Yes — within 14 days of receipt, for direct-to-consumer purchases. The product must be in its original packaging and in good cosmetic condition. Resellers may offer different return windows.

### What's the difference between Aurora-X1 and Aurora-X1 Pro?

The Pro adds a thermal imaging camera, RTK GPS for centimetre-level positioning, a stronger airframe, and an extended 38-minute flight time. The Pro is aimed at surveyors, search-and-rescue teams, and inspection professionals.
